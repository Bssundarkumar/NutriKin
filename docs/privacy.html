NutriKin privacy policy

Last updated: 27 September 2026

NutriKin ("the app") is operated by Satya Sundar Kumar Bandiguptapu. Contact: bssundarkumar@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Account: your email address, used to sign you in with a one-time code.
  • Family profile you enter: member names, and optionally age, sex, height, weight, health conditions, allergies and nutrition goals. This is health-related information.
  • Scan history: the products you scan (barcode, name, brand) and the score each family member received.
  • Apple Health (optional): if you connect it, the app reads weight, blood glucose, blood pressure and calories on your device. This data stays on your device and is not uploaded or written back to Health.
  • Camera and photos: used on your device to read barcodes and labels. These images are processed on the device and are not uploaded.
  • AI features (optional): on iPhones that support Apple Intelligence, the chat and meal ideas run entirely on your iPhone using Apple's on-device model, and nothing is sent anywhere. If you link your own AI account (an API key for Claude by Anthropic, OpenAI, Grok by xAI, or Gemini by Google), you can also scan a plate, have a product label read from your photos, and get AI-suggested alternatives, and you can choose to use that AI for chat and meal ideas. When you use one of those, your photo or question is sent directly from your iPhone to that company, under your own account, together with the family details needed to answer (member names, ages, height and weight, conditions, allergies and goals that you entered). NutriKin's servers never receive these requests or your key. The key is kept only in your iPhone's secure Keychain and is removed when you disconnect, sign out or delete your account. That company's handling of the data is described in its own privacy policy. Free tiers of some AI services may use submitted data to improve their products, so check the terms of the service you link. Do not include other people or documents in photos.

How we use it

Only to run the app: to sign you in, share your family's profiles between the family's phones, calculate scores and show your history. We do not sell your data, do not use it for advertising, and do not track you across other apps or websites.

Where it is stored and who else sees it

  • Account and family data is stored with our backend provider, Supabase, protected by access rules so that only members of your family can read it.
  • To find product information, the app sends the barcode or product name you searched for to Open Food Facts (a public database). No account information is sent.
  • Other people in your family, whom you invite with a code, can see the family's members and scan history.

Your choices

  • Delete your account: in the app go to Family, Account, Delete account. This permanently removes your login, and the family's members and scan history if you are its only member.
  • Edit or remove any family member or scan at any time inside the app.
  • Access to your data or questions: email us at the address above.

Not medical advice

NutriKin provides general information only and is not a medical device. Product data can be incomplete or wrong; always check the package label, especially for allergies.

Children

The app is intended for adults, who may add children as family members. We do not knowingly create accounts for children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its date.
