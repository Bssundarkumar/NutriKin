NutriKin support

NutriKin scans food barcodes or labels and shows how each product fits the health needs of every person in your family.

Questions, bugs or data requests: bssundarkumar@gmail.com

Common questions

How do I delete my account? In the app: Family tab, Account, Delete account. This permanently removes your login and, if you are the only person in your family, the family's members and scan history.

How do I join a family? Ask a family member for the invite code (Family tab), then choose "Join with a code" after signing in.

Is this medical advice? No. NutriKin gives general guidance from the information you enter and public product data. Always check the package label, especially for allergies, and ask a doctor or dietitian about medical decisions.
