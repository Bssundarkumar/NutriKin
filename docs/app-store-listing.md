**Description:**
NutriKin checks food for everyone you feed.

Add your family members with their allergies and health needs. Then scan a product's barcode, or photograph its label, and NutriKin scores it for each person, in plain language.

- Family profiles: age, sex, conditions such as diabetes, high blood pressure or high cholesterol, allergies (including your own custom ones) and daily goals.
- Instant scores: a clear "good", "okay" or "avoid" for each person, with the reasons behind it.
- Allergy protection: a product containing someone's allergen is always flagged.
- Ingredient alerts: additives and ingredients widely advised to limit, explained simply, with quantities where the label states them.
- No barcode? Take a photo of the ingredients or the front of the pack and confirm the product.
- Scan a plate (optional): link your own AI account to estimate a meal's calories from a photo, with each family member's share of their daily limits.
- Ask AI about any scanned product, and get a day of meal ideas for each person, sized to their plan and free of their allergens.
- BMI-based target weight and suggested daily intake for adults.
- Nutri-Score and NOVA grades, and better-scoring alternatives.
- Scan history shared across your family's phones.

Product information comes from Open Food Facts, a free, community-built food database.

NutriKin gives general guidance only. It is not medical advice and does not replace a doctor or dietitian. Product data can be incomplete, so always check the package label, especially for allergies.
